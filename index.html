Precision Thinking for a Complex World

Mechanism-based research on AI, institutions, political economy, technological change, and human adaptation.

Precision Analytica studies the mechanisms behind complex social, technological, institutional, and economic change. The work emphasizes explanation over noise, structure over slogans, and disciplined inquiry into how systems evolve.

Research Architecture

Research Architecture

The research program is organized around mechanisms that connect AI, institutions, social structure, technological change, markets, and human adaptation.

AI, Institutions, and Human Formation

How AI changes capability formation, delegation, judgment, education, work, family, and the institutional processes that reproduce human skills.

Social Structure and Political Economy

How middle-class stability, dignity, institutional lag, visibility systems, and polarization reshape modern political and social order.

Technology, Growth, and Civilization

How technological waves, institutional operating systems, capital, education, and social transmission shape long-run growth and civilizational adaptation.

Markets, Risk, and Diffusion

How markets absorb shocks, how investor capacity shapes adjustment, and how innovations diffuse through institutions and carriers.

About

About Precision Analytica

Precision Analytica is an independent research platform focused on mechanism-based analysis of complex systems. The work combines economics, political economy, institutional analysis, systems thinking, and applied modeling to explain how patterns emerge and how they evolve.
