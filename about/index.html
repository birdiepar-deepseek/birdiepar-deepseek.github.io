About

Precision Analytica

Precision Analytica is the research home of Arthur Claudino, an economist and independent researcher studying artificial intelligence, institutions, knowledge systems, and technological change.

The site publishes research notes, working papers, and essays that examine how emerging technologies reshape human organization, economic behavior, social coordination, and long-run institutional adaptation.

Arthur Claudino

Arthur Claudino develops conceptual frameworks for understanding how artificial intelligence reshapes knowledge, institutions, and human organization.

His work draws on applied economics, political economy, institutional analysis, and formal modeling. Current projects examine knowledge convergence, AI and human expertise, institutional adaptation, social coordination, capability erosion, and the economic consequences of technological disruption.

Research Approach

The work here is organized around mechanisms rather than news cycles.

A typical project begins with a puzzle: a social, economic, or technological pattern that existing language does not fully capture. From there, the research develops concepts, essays, models, and working papers that make the mechanism easier to analyze.

The goal is not to predict every technological outcome, but to build a vocabulary for understanding how human systems change under pressure.

Publications

Precision Analytica publishes three main types of work:

Research Notes - shorter essays introducing ideas, mechanisms, and conceptual frameworks.

Working Papers - formal papers with theoretical models, empirical evidence, or extended arguments. Selected working papers are available by request.

Book Projects and Essays - longer synthesis work on AI, institutions, education, work, family, and the future of human society.
