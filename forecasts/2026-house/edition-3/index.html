Los Angeles, 17 October 2026 — Relative to the currently seated House—214 Democrats, 218 Republicans, one independent and two vacancies—the projection adds ten Democratic seats and changes chamber control.

October generic-ballot averages are near D+8.5. The model adopts a more cautious D+7.4 election-vote scenario, allowing for midterm turnout uncertainty and diminishing gains in closely contested districts. The 223–226 seat range reflects alternative assumptions and unresolved close races.
