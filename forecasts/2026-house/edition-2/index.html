Los Angeles, 11 September 2026 — Precision Analytica retains its July forecast of 221 Democratic seats and 214 Republican seats in the U.S. House of Representatives, with a published Democratic range of 220 to 223 seats under a D+5.5 national two-party House vote scenario.

Edition 2 incorporates completed primaries, confirmed general-election matchups and district polling released since the first edition. These updates do not materially change the chamber-level forecast.

The model continues to place the national two-party House vote required for control at approximately D+3½ on the enacted maps. Arizona's 6th District remains the decisive 218th seat in the central seat ladder.

Next update. Precision Analytica plans its next forecast update in October, when a broader body of post-primary general-election polling will be available.

Methodology note

The Electoral Conversion Model translates changes in the national two-party House vote into district-level outcomes using district-specific electoral baselines and transmission estimates. Full methodology, sources and sensitivity analysis are available in the accompanying research note.
