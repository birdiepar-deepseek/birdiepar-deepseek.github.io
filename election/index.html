This prototype currently identifies Texas Senate as the clearest structural-risk race. Most House races remain low-signal because public district-level polling is sparse and several nomination contests are unresolved. The dashboard therefore separates actual structural signals from missing-data uncertainty.

Standard election forecasts capture the conventional race environment. This public dashboard highlights where those forecasts may be fragile because of structural turnout, candidate, coordination, endorsement, polling-screen, and missing-data risks.

What Surprise Risk Means

A surprise-risk score is not a win probability. A score of 55 does not mean a candidate has a 55% chance of winning. It is a 0-100 forecast-fragility score that highlights where conventional expectations may be more vulnerable to structural change or missing data.

Signal vs Missing Data

Some races are risky because the dashboard sees structural evidence. Other races are risky because public data is still thin. Those are different kinds of uncertainty.
