This public site intentionally omits detailed internal reports, raw inputs, source logs, and analyst-assumption files. The public version is designed to show the topline model outputs, charts, methodology, and limitations without exposing private working files.

This is an experimental public structural-risk dashboard from Precision Analytica.

  • Not campaign advice.
  • Not voter targeting.
  • Not a replacement for polling averages.
  • Not a deterministic election forecast.
  • Data gaps remain, especially in early-cycle House polling and unresolved nomination contests.
  • The dashboard tracks where standard expectations may be fragile; it does not tell campaigns whom to contact or how to persuade voters.
