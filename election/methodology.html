Baseline Layer

The baseline layer summarizes conventional race context such as ratings, incumbency, prior results, and finance inputs.

Structural Shock Framework

The dashboard tracks turnout composition, cohort activation, Trump endorsement effects, candidate/local shocks, party coordination, polling-screen gaps, and missing-data uncertainty.

TCSI

Turnout Composition Shock Index indicates whether turnout composition evidence may stress baseline assumptions.

CAI

Cohort Activation Index tracks race-specific age or generation activation evidence. It remains neutral when evidence is insufficient.

TESI

Trump Endorsement Shock Index separates primary coordination effects from possible general-election backlash.

CLSI

Candidate / Local Shock Index captures candidate-quality, local issue, recruitment, or legal/scandal risk when source-backed.

Overall Surprise Risk

Overall surprise risk combines structural signals and missing-data uncertainty. It ranks forecast fragility, not candidate probability.

Data Classes

Inputs are separated into observed data, proxy signals, analyst assumptions, and model outputs.

Limitations

Public outputs omit detailed internal reports and raw manual data. Missing data can remain substantial before the general election.

Structural-risk indicators, not deterministic election forecasts.
